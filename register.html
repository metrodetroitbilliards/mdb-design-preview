TEAM REGISTRATION.

Register your qualified team using the league's roster system.

Before you start

  1. Tap Open team registration below. The registration screen opens first.
  2. Choose your format, then your qualified team and check its remaining entries.
  3. Review your eligible players. Rosters of 5–8 fill automatically; choose 5–8 if more are available. Confirm your roster before submitting.

Each successful registration uses one earned entry in that format. If entries remain, select the team again to submit another roster; the players may be different.

This is the active roster form.Submitting sends information to the league's registration system. This page does not collect payment.
