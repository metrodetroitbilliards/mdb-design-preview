TEAM REGISTRATION.

Register your qualified team using the league's roster system.

Before you start

  1. Open registration and tap Team Registration at the top—not Log Match.
  2. Choose your format, then your qualified team and bid.
  3. Select 5–8 players and check your roster before submitting.

Register each team bid separately for each format, even when the players are the same.

This is the active roster form.Submitting sends information to the league's registration system. This page does not collect payment.
