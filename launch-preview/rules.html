USAPL

-All Ages welcome under 18 welcome with parent or guardian

-Requires a minimum of 5 players and a maximum of 8 on a roster.

-Womens Divisions require 3 players and a maximum of 5 on a roster with a 1,275 Fargo Cap

-2,375 Team Fargo Cap for a 5-Player team (going over the cap is minus 1 point from the team total for each point over the cap)

-USAPL Short Races

-USAPL 14pt scoring system

Team standings are based off of Points Per Set or PPS.

Have a BYE week? No problem!

With this setup it only equates the weeks you have played as PPS is the average number of points per set.

For example, your team scored 500 points for the night, 500 points / 5 sets = 100 PPS

Number of Weeks Played x 5 = Number of Sets Played

Total Points ÷ Number of Sets Played = Total PPS

Currently, there are no annual dues for USAPL members.

In single play divisions, weekly dues are $9 per set and play 5 sets per week.

In double play divisions, weekly dues are $8 per set and play 10 sets per week.

New USAPL Players; First 2 sets are FREE

New USAPL Captains; First 4 sets are FREE

BCAPL

-Has variable team formatting but our primary divisions will be our 3x3x3 8, 9, & 10-Ball

-Requires a minimum of 3 players and a maximum of 5 on a roster

-Each player plays 9 racks in total, 3 in each discipline against different opponents

-No Timeouts

-No team Fargo Cap

-Matches are handicapped based on team fargo totals

-$20 BCAPL Membership dues (renews on 1/1)

-70% payout at end of session

-20% held for the year end event

-Payouts are based on # of wins

-# of weeks is dependant on number of teams, normally 2-3 round-robins

Makeups

-Teams must contact their opponent in captains group text at least two hours before the start of league play. If done later than that, it will be the option of the opposing team whether to opt for makeup, pick one of the players to play twice, or possibly even forfeit if they have 3 or less players

-If a team has to forfeit their opponents they are awarded 100 points per set that is forfeited.

-We want to keep make up matches to a single match but understand there are times that a team may run into issues and need multiple makeups during a session. They MUST contact us by email for approval and to ensure make ups are done in a timely manner.

-Make ups must be done before the last week of the session, unless otherwise approved by the league operator.

-Additional details on the makeup process can be found here

Forfeits

Teams that forfeit a match without providing the required 2-hour notice are still responsible for their portion of the weekly dues. This ensures the prize fund remains whole for the end-of-session payouts.

Player Unsportsmanlike Conduct & Etiquette

~Continued unsportsmanlike conduct will not be tolerated. Everyone can have a bad night but please try not to take it out on others as everyone comes to league to have a good time. If you have a concern/complaint, please email us with any details you can provide.

~"Sharking" (including loud talking during a shot, standing in the line of sight, saying good game as a player goes to line up for a shot, or hovering over a pocket) is considered unsportsmanlike conduct.

1st offence will result in a warning or suspension depending on severity

2nd offence will result in a suspension or removal from the league depending on severity

3rd offence will be removal from the league or banned from all CSI/USA/BCA Pool Leagues depending on severity

~The # of offences stays on for one year before dropping off one.

~Official protests regarding a specific match or rule interpretation must be submitted via email within 24–48 hours of the match in question. The League Operator and PAB will review and provide a final decision by the following Sunday.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: What happens if our team is over the Fargo Cap? A: For the USAPL divisions, if your team's starting Fargo total exceeds the cap, a penalty of 1 point per point over the cap is applied to your team's total score for that match.

Q: How do "Makeups" work if we can’t play on our scheduled night? A: You must notify the opposing captain and the League Manager as soon as possible. Makeups must be completed within 2 weeks of the original date. You can "double up" (play two matches on one night) or find an alternative night that works for both teams.

Q: Can I play for more than one team? A: Players are generally restricted to one team per division to ensure fair play and scheduling. If you wish to sub for another team, please consult the PAB or League Manager for specific session eligibility.

Q: What are "Greens Fees" and do I always have to pay them? A: Greens fees cover the cost of table time at the host establishment. These vary by location (e.g., Ypsilanti Moose, Grizzlys Wyandotte, etc.). Check the "Locations" section of the bylaws for the specific fee at your venue.

Q: How are session awards determined? A: Awards are based on a point system tied to your final standings and the number of teams in your division. Once the session ends, captains use the League Award Form to select items like hoodies, jackets, or trophies based on their earned points.

Q: What is the Player Advisory Board (PAB) and how can I join? A: The PAB is a group of players who help settle disputes and vote on rule changes. Members rotate every year. If you are interested in joining, look for the nomination announcement sent out via email/text at the end of the Fall session.

Q: Does my BCAPL membership renew automatically? A: No. BCAPL memberships ($20) renew annually on January 1st. You must pay your dues at the start of the first session of the new year to remain sanctioned.
