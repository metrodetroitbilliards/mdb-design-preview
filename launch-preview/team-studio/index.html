A few things to know.

Is the designer free?

Yes. Customize these templates as often as you like. Shirt production is priced separately by MDB.

Can I get a different graphic or a special design?

Add your idea in the request message. These are editable logo templates, not an AI image generator. MDB will confirm what can be produced.

Can I download a print file?

This studio is for shirts ordered through MDB. Production artwork stays in the MDB order workflow; there is no public print-file download.

Is my design saved?

When browser storage is available, your latest design stays on this device. The request also includes a design link so MDB can reopen the same choices. Clearing browser data removes your local draft.
