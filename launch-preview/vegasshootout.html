HOW THE TEAM EVENT WORKS

Two stages. One champion.

Stage 1: Round robin / group play

Your team plays its scheduled group matches rather than being eliminated after one loss. Every match matters: your results build your standing and determine whether you advance. Complete all of your scheduled matches and record the results using the Keep Score button above.

Stage 2: Single-elimination bracket

Teams that advance enter the final bracket. Win your match to move to the next round; lose a match and your run in that bracket ends. Play continues until a champion is decided.

In short: Play your group matches → qualify for the final bracket → win to advance.

Stage 2 starts at 10 AM Sunday: November 8 for Week 1 and November 15 for Week 2. All times Eastern.

Follow the posted Vegas Shootout schedule for match times and tables. League staff will confirm advancing teams, bracket positions and any tiebreakers. This overview is for the team event, not the separate cash events.

  3. Review your eligible players. Rosters of 5–8 fill automatically; choose 5–8 if more are available. Confirm your roster before submitting.

Each successful registration uses one earned entry in that format. If entries remain, select the team again to submit another roster; the players may be different.
